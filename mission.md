LifeTime Fishing Fund
Vision & Organizational Scope
Mission Statement

The LifeTime Fishing Fund is a mission-driven company dedicated to expanding fishing access, supporting conservation, and building a transparent community funding model that strengthens outdoor heritage for future generations.

Vision Statement

The LifeTime Fishing Fund envisions a future where every person has the opportunity to responsibly participate in fishing, hunting, and outdoor recreation while contributing to the conservation and stewardship of the natural resources that make those experiences possible.

Through education, community support, accessible resources, and transparent funding initiatives, we work to strengthen outdoor heritage across the United States and its territories for present and future generations.

Organizational Purpose

The LifeTime Fishing Fund exists to support participation in fishing, hunting, outdoor recreation, conservation, and stewardship through programs, educational resources, community partnerships, and public information initiatives aligned with its mission.

The organization seeks to reduce barriers to participation, promote responsible use of natural resources, and support efforts that strengthen outdoor traditions and conservation awareness.

Geographic Scope

The LifeTime Fishing Fund is focused on serving communities throughout the United States and its territories.

The organization may develop programs, educational materials, public information resources, partnerships, and community initiatives that support outdoor participation, conservation awareness, and responsible stewardship across these regions.

Core Principles
Access

Responsible outdoor participation should be available to as many people as possible.

Conservation

Healthy ecosystems are essential to future outdoor opportunities.

Stewardship

Natural resources should be managed responsibly for the benefit of current and future generations.

Education

Knowledge and understanding strengthen conservation outcomes and encourage responsible participation.

Transparency

Community support should be met with accountability, openness, and responsible management.

Legacy

The decisions made today should help preserve outdoor opportunities for those who follow.

Long-Term Vision

The LifeTime Fishing Fund seeks to build a lasting network of educational resources, conservation information, community partnerships, and access initiatives that preserve fishing, hunting, and outdoor heritage while promoting responsible management of America's natural resources.

The organization recognizes that communities, technologies, and outdoor activities will continue to evolve. By strengthening stewardship, education, and conservation awareness today, the Fund seeks to create lasting value for future generations.

Closing Statement

We believe conservation succeeds when people have meaningful access to the outdoors.

We believe education strengthens stewardship.

We believe responsible participation creates advocates for conservation.

We believe outdoor heritage deserves preservation.

And we believe that every generation shares a responsibility to leave future generations with greater knowledge, stronger stewardship, and broader opportunities than those inherited from the last.
